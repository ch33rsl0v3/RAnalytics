Taking off now

Grouped by how far other developers have got with the theme, untouched first, strongest first inside each group. Each game is logged the day it first appears and checked after 21 days: see Was it right?

Nothing up to 30 days old has heat of 3,500 or more right now. That is an answer, not a fault: most days nothing is taking off. Lowering min_heat in config.json widens the net and the hit rate drops with it.
